Frequently asked questions

Before we begin.

Is NEXORA a large agency?

NEXORA is an emerging creative brand. We communicate transparently about our capacity and only accept work that can be delivered to the agreed standard.

Do you use AI?

AI-assisted tools may be used for concept development, visuals, or parts of production when appropriate. The exact workflow depends on the project, and client assets are handled according to agreed terms.

Are the portfolio projects real client campaigns?

No. The AURELLE SKIN, VOLTARA, and VELMONT examples shown here are fictional concept projects. They are not endorsements, client commissions, or evidence of campaign results.

How many revisions are included?

Each package lists its included revision rounds. A revision adjusts the approved direction; a new concept, new script after approval, or additional deliverable may require a separate quote.

Can you guarantee sales or viral views?

No. Advertising performance depends on many factors beyond the creative asset. NEXORA focuses on delivering the agreed creative work and does not guarantee sales, views, or return on ad spend.

Can you work with international businesses?

Yes, projects can be discussed remotely. Before work begins, both sides should confirm payment method, currency, deadlines, usage rights, and any applicable fees.
